What scraping 1,000 fake books from a website taught me

By Navya Asopa

While the website Books to Scrape looks like a typical online bookstore, it’s a sandbox for students like me to practice web scraping. I extracted key fields of the book’s title, genre, price range, rating, and availability using Python to fetch them, BeautifulSoup to parse them, and by looping through 50 listing pages to collect them.

Then, I used Pandas to group the data by genre and rating. Finally, I cleaned the data and stored it in a CSV file, using Datawrapper to visualize my analysis. After analyzing a database of 1,000 books, I’ve drawn some preliminary conclusions about book prices, their most common genre, and the average price of books within each genre.

1. Price Distribution of Books

Most books fall into a mid-range bracket, forming a loose bell-shaped distribution on the chart, as only a few are either too expensive or too economical. The largest group, i.e. 113 books, are priced at £35-40.

2. Percentage of Books by Genre

This chart was challenging because the dataset had two book columns: “Default” and “Add a comment”. The former were the uncategorized books, and the latter resulted from an error in the scraped website's backend HTML. So, I clubbed the two and named the group “Uncategorized,” which ended up occupying the largest percentage, 24%, in the genre section after “Others,” including young adult, fantasy, romance, and mystery, among others. A fair share of books belong to clearly defined genres such as Fiction, Nonfiction, and Sequential Art.

3. Average Price by Genre

The top-priced genres are Travel (£39.8), Fantasy (£39.6), and History (£37.3), while the lower-priced genres are Food & Drink (£31.4), Thriller (£31.4), and Mystery (£31.7). Although there is some variation in prices, most genres cluster within a fairly narrow range of ~£31–£40. This means that no genre is dramatically more pricey than others.

4. Book Ratings by Genre

Poetry (3.53) has the highest average rating, followed by humor and young adult. These genres create emotional value and might have received more favorable reader reviews compared to Philosophy (2.2), Science Fiction (2.25), and Classic (2.47), which are denser to read. These numbers could also be dictated by fewer rankings in a genre. While most genres cluster between 2.7 and 3.3, the differences can be meaningful for gauging readers' opinions of book genres.
